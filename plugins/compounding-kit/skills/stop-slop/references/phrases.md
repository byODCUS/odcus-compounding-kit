# Banned Phrases

## German AI markers (added 2026-07-14, research: $WORKSPACE/guides/linkedin-ai-marker-reichweite-research-2026-07.md)

### Wörter (Signifikanz-Inflation / Marketing-Tics)
- entscheidend / essenziell (as significance claim without evidence)
- vielfältig, nahtlos, massgeschneidert, ganzheitlich
- revolutionieren / revolutionär / transformativ
- "spielt eine zentrale Rolle" / "spielt eine entscheidende Rolle"
- "im digitalen Zeitalter" / "in der heutigen digitalen Welt"
- Game-Changer (Anglicism in German text)
- zukunftssicher, innovativ (as self-description)
- wirklich / echt / tatsächlich as emphasis inflation ("was wirklich zählt", "die echte Frage") → cut the word or replace with the specific point. Especially in headers (see structures.md, German formulaic headers).

### Floskeln (bridges without content)
- "Es ist wichtig zu beachten, dass..."
- "Ein weiterer wichtiger Aspekt ist..."
- "Insgesamt lässt sich festhalten..."
- "Zusammenfassend kann gesagt werden..."
- "Darüber hinaus / Des Weiteren / Zudem" as mechanical paragraph openers
- "Tauchen wir ein..." / "Tauchen Sie ein in..."
- "Es empfiehlt sich..."
- "Je nach Anwendungsfall..."

### Gehörte-Sätze-Opener (HARD BAN, owner feedback 2026-11-02: "Jeder KI-Post schreibt das")
Never quote or paraphrase a sentence "heard" from clients, sessions, workshops or meetings as a hook or framing. Applies anywhere in the piece, also as a single sentence.
- "Den Satz höre ich in fast jeder Sitzung / von Kunden / ständig..."
- "Diesen Satz höre ich..." / "Ein Satz, den ich oft höre..." / "Kürzlich sagte mir ein Kunde..."
- "Wenn ich mit Geschäftsführern spreche, höre ich fast immer dasselbe"
- "In vielen Gesprächen höre ich..." / "Immer wieder höre ich..."
- "Fast jeder Kunde fragt mich..." / "Die häufigste Frage, die ich bekomme..."
- English: "I hear this all the time", "A client told me last week...", "The question I get most..."
Fix: state the point directly, or give the real, specific own moment (date, number, what happened), not a reported quote.

### Wochentag-Szenen-Opener (HARD BAN, owner feedback 2026-11-02: "another no go")
Never set a scene with a generic weekday or time slot. It is an invented vignette dressed up as a real moment. Applies to headings, hooks and body text.
- Headings like "Der Dienstag", "Ein Montag im Büro", "Freitagnachmittag"
- "Am Dienstagmorgen sitzt die Geschäftsführerin...", "Nächsten Montag...", "Ein typischer Freitag..."
- "Es ist Mittwoch, 8 Uhr...", "Montagmorgen, Posteingang voll..."
- English: "A Tuesday", "Next Monday", "A typical Friday", "It's 9 a.m. on a Wednesday..."
Fix: state the situation directly ("Ein Ingenieurbüro mit 60 Leuten sieht diesen Herbst drei KI-Demos."), or use a real, dated own moment.

### German structural checks
- Sentence-length variance: AI German clusters at 15-25 words/sentence; aim for 6-40 spread
- Nominalization chains ("die Entwicklung der Transformation der...") → rewrite verb-forward
- Hedging pile-ups (kann / könnte / es empfiehlt sich) → take a position
- Vague examples ("ein mittelständisches Unternehmen") → use a concrete anonymized case
- Adjective triples without evidence ("umfassend, ganzheitlich, nachhaltig")

## Throat-clearing openers
Remove these — they announce content instead of delivering it.

- "Here's the thing:"
- "The truth is"
- "The reality is"
- "Here's what's interesting:"
- "Spoiler:"
- "Hot take:"
- "Unpopular opinion:"
- "Let me be clear:"
- "To be fair:"
- "At the end of the day"
- "When all is said and done"
- "Make no mistake"
- "The fact of the matter is"
- "Here's the deal:"
- "Bottom line:"

## Faux-insight setups (added 2026-07-23, source: petergyang/no-ai-slop)
Announce that an insight is coming instead of delivering it. Delete the setup; let the claim stand alone.

- "What nobody tells you..."
- "This is what most people skip/miss/get wrong"
- "The part everyone overlooks"
- German: "Was dir niemand sagt...", "Was die meisten übersehen..."

## Emphasis crutches
Add no meaning — delete.

- "Full stop."
- "Period."
- "Let that sink in."
- "Read that again."
- "This is huge."
- "I cannot stress this enough."
- "This changes everything."
- "This is a big deal."

## Meta-commentary
References to the text itself — disrupts flow.

- "In this section, we'll..."
- "As I mentioned above..."
- "Let me walk you through..."
- "I'll explain this later..."
- "By the end of this..."

## Performative emphasis
False intimacy.

- "I promise."
- "Trust me."
- "Believe it or not."
- "You won't believe..."

## Vague declaratives
Claim significance without specifics — replace with the actual fact.

- "The implications are significant."
- "This is worth noting."
- "This has major consequences."
- "The results speak for themselves."
- "Numbers don't lie."
- "The data is clear."
- "History shows us that..."
- "As we know,"
- "It goes without saying that"
- "Needless to say,"

## Weasel attribution (added 2026-07-23, source: petergyang/no-ai-slop)
Vague authority instead of a named source. Ties to Tier 1 (no statistic without a
verified source): replace with the named source and link, or cut the claim.

- "Experts agree..."
- "Studies show..." / "Research suggests..."
- "Many believe..." / "It is widely known..."
- German: "Experten sind sich einig...", "Studien zeigen..." (ohne Quelle), "Viele glauben..."

## Summary-recap endings — English (added 2026-07-23, source: petergyang/no-ai-slop)
German equivalents already listed under Floskeln above. Cut the recap; end on a
concrete point, takeaway, or next action.

- "In conclusion," / "To conclude,"
- "In summary," / "To sum up," / "All in all,"
- "Ultimately, ..." as a wrap-up paragraph opener
- Any final paragraph that restates prior paragraphs without adding anything

## Significance inflation — English markers (folded in from /humanizer)
Puff up importance without evidence. English complement to the German Signifikanz-Inflation list above.

- stands/serves as a testament/reminder
- a pivotal/vital/crucial/key moment / turning point
- marking/shaping/setting the stage for
- reflects a broader trend, evolving landscape, focal point
- indelible mark, deeply rooted, enduring legacy

## Promotional / advertisement language (folded in from /humanizer)
Brochure tone. Neutral facts instead.

- nestled, in the heart of, boasts a, vibrant, rich (figurative)
- breathtaking, stunning, must-visit, renowned, groundbreaking (figurative)
- showcasing, exemplifies, commitment to, seamless, natural beauty

## Pasted-chatbot artifacts (folded in from /humanizer)
Assistant-correspondence text left inside content. Delete outright.

- "Great question!", "Certainly!", "Of course!", "You're absolutely right"
- "I hope this helps", "Let me know if you'd like…", "Here is a…", "Would you like…"
- Sycophancy: "That's an excellent point", "What a fascinating topic"
- Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers: "As of my last update", "While specific details are limited/scarce", "based on available information"
